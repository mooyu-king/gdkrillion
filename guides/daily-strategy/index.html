Daily strategy

Ten short prompts can jump between very different parts of Geometry Dash. A steady reading and typing routine makes it easier to change categories during the daily set.

Before you start the set

Choose Rare or Crowd while the game is on its start screen. If you are on a phone, consider your preferred orientation before opening a timed prompt. Keeping the game stable while the keyboard opens makes it easier to see the answer field.

Read the restriction first

Find the words that narrow the category, then think of a candidate. Watch for a required rating, a time limit on a published list, or a split between platformer and classic levels. Repeat the condition mentally before typing a name you associate with the broader topic.

Choose while you still have time to type

If a rarer name comes to mind with uncertain spelling, compare it to the reliable answer you already have. Leave enough of the thirty seconds to enter one clear response. The game accepts some alternate spellings, but typing a name you can confidently recognize gives you a better starting point.

Take one lesson into the next daily

When a prompt catches you off guard, note the category after the reveal. Practice recalling two examples during a later play session. If your answers regularly land in an unexpected part of the distribution, revisit the Rare vs. Crowd guide before the next set.
