Rare vs. Crowd

Every prompt in GD Krillion has a category. It also has a second question you answer privately: which of my valid answers will other people reach for? Rare and Crowd reverse the payoff for that second judgment.

Start with two candidates

Picture a hypothetical prompt about levels that feature a certain mechanic. Think of a level lots of players know and a second level you remember from a smaller creator. Confirm that both actually use the mechanic. You now have two defensible choices before considering how often each might appear.

Pick for Rare

Choose the less frequently remembered answer if you are confident it fits. Familiarity from your own play history can make an uncommon answer easy to retrieve. A small creator you follow or a section you practiced repeatedly can give you that edge.

Pick for Crowd

Imagine that a player who knows the game well enough to recognize the category has only a few seconds to think. A famous showcase, introductory example or widely discussed creator is a good candidate if it meets every condition in the prompt.

Use the reveal to recalibrate

If an answer felt obscure and appeared higher in the distribution than you expected, consider where other players encountered it. A widely shared video or a list category can make an answer familiar within the exact group playing the daily trivia. Try the sample answer reveal to see how a hypothetical ranking reverses.
