Privacy Policy

This policy describes the GD Krillion Online website. Effective November 24, 2026.

Traffic measurement

This site loads Plausible analytics through the shared data.1back.link service to understand page visits and use of installation features. Requests may include ordinary browser and network information. Our site does not send your game answers or include private input in analytics event properties.

Hosted game

Choosing to play loads a separate website in the game panel. That provider handles game requests and gameplay data under its own practices. The site around the game does not read your answers, results or game storage.

Local app storage

A service worker can cache published pages, styles, scripts and illustrations on your device. A short-lived session flag keeps installation-use measurement from repeating on every page during a browser session. You can clear the local cache and session data in your browser settings. The cache does not include the third-party game.
