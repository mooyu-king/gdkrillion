Site information

About Us

GD Krillion Online offers one place to open the daily Geometry Dash trivia game and learn how its answer scoring works. The play page embeds a separately hosted game. The guides and illustrations on this site are made for this site.

What you can do here

Start the daily set, practice with a sample answer distribution, or follow a short guide before you play. The sample figures on the homepage are illustrative. The daily questions, accepted answers and scores come from the hosted game.

Editorial approach

We use original illustrations and examples in the guides, and aim to keep the current daily answers out of them. The project is independently operated. Game names used to describe the trivia subject belong to their respective owners.
